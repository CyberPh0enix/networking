Interfaces & Ports

  • IP = The Building: Routes the packet to the correct machine on the network.
  • Port = The Apartment: (0-65535) Routes the packet to the specific App (e.g. 25565 for Minecraft).
  • 127.0.0.1 (Localhost): App listens only to internal OS. Highly secure.
  • 0.0.0.0 (Any): App listens on ALL network interfaces (Wi-Fi, Ethernet).

Network Quality Metrics

  • Bandwidth: The width of the highway (e.g. 1 Gbps capacity).
  • Latency (Ping): Time for a packet to travel and return (20ms).
  • Jitter: Variance in latency. If ping jumps from 20ms to 150ms, that's high jitter (rubberbanding).

VPNs & The Privacy Paradox

WireGuard vs OpenVPN

OpenVPN: Old, user-space, heavy overhead.
WireGuard: Next-gen, runs directly in Linux kernel (`wg0`), stealthy (drops unauth packets silently).

The Egress Risk

VPNs encrypt the tunnel, but the VPN Provider acts as your new ISP. They can see all DNS and TLS SNI metadata.

How Censorship & DPI Work

How does an ISP actually enforce a ban on apps like Telegram?

1

DNS Spoofing

You ask for telegram.org. The ISP's DNS lies and returns 0.0.0.0.

2

SNI Filtering & Deep Packet Inspection (DPI)

Even on HTTPS, the initial "Server Name" is plain text. The firewall reads it, sees a banned domain, and injects a TCP RST (Reset) packet to kill the connection.

3

BGP Blackholing

ISPs advertise false routes for the target's IP blocks, routing national traffic into a digital void.
